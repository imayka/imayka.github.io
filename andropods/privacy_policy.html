AndroPods — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026 · App: AndroPods (pro.vitalii.andropods) on Google Play

This policy explains what data the AndroPods Android app ("the app") accesses, collects, uses and shares, and the choices you have. The app is developed and published by Vitalii Parkhomenko ("I", "me"), an individual developer. For any privacy question or request, write to dev@vitalii.pro.

AndroPods is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Apple Inc. AirPods, AirPods Pro and Powerbeats Pro are trademarks of Apple Inc. or its affiliates; they are named here only to describe which devices the app works with.

Summary

  • The app has no user accounts and no ads. It does not sell your data.
  • Battery levels, Bluetooth data, your contacts and your settings are processed on your device only and are not sent to me.
  • The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous usage statistics (described below), and Google Play Billing for the optional Pro purchase.

Data processed on your device only

Data / permissionWhy the app needs itWhat happens with it
Bluetooth (Nearby devices; on Android 11 and older also Location, including in the background while the service is enabled) To receive the Bluetooth Low Energy broadcasts in which wireless earbuds announce their battery level, charging state and in-ear state. Android 11 and older only allow Bluetooth scanning with the Location permission. On Android 12+ the app declares that it does not use Bluetooth scans to derive location. Broadcasts are read in memory, filtered to supported earbuds, and discarded. Broadcasts of other nearby devices are ignored. The app never reads GPS or your location, and nothing is stored or transmitted, except the last battery levels of your own earbuds, which are kept in the app's settings on your device to show them in the notification.
Contacts (optional) Only if you enable "call a contact" for an earbud gesture and pick a contact. The chosen contact's name and phone number are saved in the app's settings on your device. They are never uploaded.
Phone (place calls, phone state) To call the contact you chose when you use that gesture, and to know whether a call is in progress so earbud controls behave correctly. Used on the device only; call information is not stored or transmitted.
Display over other apps To show the battery popup when you open the earbud case.No data involved.
Notifications, run at startup, foreground service, ignore battery optimization (optional) To show the battery notification and keep the service running so the popup appears reliably.No data involved.
SettingsYour preferences (style, popup options, etc.).Stored on your device in the app's private storage.

Data collected by third-party services

Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC) gives me basic statistics, such as how many people use the app and on which phones and Android versions. The app sends only the events the Firebase SDK collects automatically; it does not log any events or details of its own. It collects:

  • App activity: automatic events such as first open, app sessions, screens viewed in the app, engagement time, and app updates.
  • Device information: device model, Android version, app version, and language.
  • Identifiers: the Firebase app-instance ID, a random ID created for this installation. The app does not use the Android Advertising ID; that permission is removed from the app.
  • Approximate location: country and region derived by Google from the IP address of the request. The app itself never reads your location.

This data is not linked to your name, email or contacts and is not used for advertising. It is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and processed by Google as described in the Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Google Play Billing processes the optional Pro purchase. Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play. The app only receives the purchase status and a purchase token from Google Play, to unlock Pro features. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing

I do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, other than the processing by Google described above, which acts as a service provider for analytics and payments. Data may be disclosed if required by law.

Retention and deletion

  • On-device data (settings, chosen contact, last battery levels) stays until you change it, clear the app's data (Android Settings → Apps → AndroPods → Storage → Clear data), or uninstall the app.
  • Analytics data is kept by Google Analytics according to the data-retention setting of the app's Analytics property: user-level and event-level data for at most 14 months, after which it is deleted automatically. Only aggregated reports that do not identify an installation may be kept longer. Clearing the app's data or reinstalling the app creates a new app-instance ID, so new statistics are no longer associated with the old one.
  • You can ask me to delete analytics data associated with your installation by writing to dev@vitalii.pro. The app has no accounts, so there is no account to delete.

Security

On-device data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but I take reasonable measures to protect your data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact me and I will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU or UK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to or restrict its processing. Analytics is processed on the basis of legitimate interest in keeping the app working on as many devices as possible. To exercise any right, write to dev@vitalii.pro.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
